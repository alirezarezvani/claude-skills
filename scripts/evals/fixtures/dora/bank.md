# Hansabank AG

Credit institution authorised in Germany, supervised by BaFin. About 600,000
retail clients, 410,000 of them use online banking. Not a microenterprise.

## Incident ICT-2026-41

- Online banking and the mobile app were unavailable from Friday 2026-11-13 16:40 CET to 21:40 CET.
- Online banking supports a critical function (retail payments).
- About 75,000 clients tried and failed to make payments during the outage.
- Only clients in Germany were affected. Cost so far: about EUR 40,000.
- No sign of malicious access: a failed database certificate rotation at our hosting provider.
- We became aware at 17:00 CET and classified it as a major ICT-related incident at 19:30 CET on Friday.
- Nothing has been reported yet. It is now Friday 2026-11-13, 20:15 CET.

## Contract under review

CardSwitch Inc. (USA) runs our card authorisation, which supports a critical function.
The contract describes the services, service levels, data locations, data return on
termination and termination rights. It has no audit or inspection rights for us or BaFin,
no exit strategy or transition period, and no clause on participating in our
threat-led penetration tests. It is not yet in our register of information.
