# Portfolio (Example Health Devices B.V., Rotterdam, 180 employees)

| Product | Notes |
|---|---|
| CardioPatch | Wearable ECG patch, CE-marked class IIa medical device under the MDR |
| CardioPatch Companion app | Phone app that pairs with CardioPatch, shows a heart-rate history and lets users share it with family. Our notified body confirmed it is NOT a medical device |
| FitBand 3 | Consumer fitness band with heart-rate and sleep monitoring, not a medical device, sold in all EU states |
| hl7-lite | Our open-source HL7 parser (Apache-2.0) on GitHub. We sell an "Enterprise" build with extra connectors and 24/7 support |
| Home Hub | Smart-home hub for the CardioPatch and FitBand, launching Q2 2028. We plan to self-assess with module A against our own security spec |

Planned support periods for products launching after 2027: 5 years each (company default). FitBand bands typically stay in use 2-3 years; Home Hub about 6-8 years.
