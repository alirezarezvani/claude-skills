# DORA scope, proportionality and ICT provider oversight

> Rules last verified 2026-11-17 against the EUR-Lex texts cited below. Re-check before relying on a deadline or threshold.

From Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 (DORA) unless marked otherwise. Applies from
**17 January 2025** (Art 64).

Confidence: 🟢 legal text · 🟡 ESA guidance or Q&A (official, non-binding) · 🔴 our reading, check with counsel

## Who is in (Art 2(1))

Twenty types of financial entity, (a) to (t): credit institutions, payment
institutions (incl. PSD2-exempt), account information service providers,
e-money institutions (incl. exempt), investment firms, crypto-asset service
providers and issuers of asset-referenced tokens, CSDs, CCPs, trading venues,
trade repositories, AIFMs, UCITS management companies, data reporting service
providers, insurance and reinsurance undertakings, insurance intermediaries,
IORPs, credit rating agencies, critical benchmark administrators,
crowdfunding service providers, securitisation repositories. Point (u) adds ICT
third-party service providers. 🟢 Art 2(1)

## Who is out (Art 2(3))

AIFMs under Art 3(2) AIFMD; small insurers under Art 4 Solvency II; IORPs with
15 or fewer members in total; persons exempt under MiFID II Arts 2-3;
insurance intermediaries that are micro, small or medium-sized; post-office
giro institutions. 🟢 Art 2(3)(a)-(f)

## Simplified ICT risk management framework (Art 16)

Small and non-interconnected investment firms, PSD2-exempt payment
institutions, CRD-exempt credit institutions (if the Member State did not
apply Art 2(4)), exempt e-money institutions and small IORPs apply Art 16
instead of Arts 5-15. They still report major incidents (Art 19), keep a
register and meet Art 30. 🟢 Art 16(1) · 🔴 on what remains

Microenterprises get lighter rules in several provisions (for example no
recurring-incident check), not an exemption. 🟢 RTS 2024/1772 Art 8(2) · 🔴 check each article for its microenterprise carve-out

## NIS2 overlap

DORA is lex specialis to NIS2 for financial entities: its ICT risk management
and incident reporting replace NIS2 Arts 21 and 23. 🟢 DORA Art 1(2); NIS2 Art 4.
Member States may still require copies of DORA reports to the NIS2 CSIRT. 🟢 DORA Art 19(1).
Entities identified as NIS2 essential or important lose weekend relief for
initial and intermediate reports. 🟢 RTS 2025/301 Art 5(5)

## Critical ICT third-party service providers (Arts 31-44)

- The ESAs designate CTPPs on criteria such as systemic impact and substitutability; first designations were made in 2025. 🟢 Art 31(2) · 🟡 ESA announcements
- A financial entity may use a CTPP established in a third country only if that provider sets up an EU subsidiary within 12 months of designation. 🟢 Art 31(12)
- The Lead Overseer can impose periodic penalty payments of up to 1% of the provider's average daily worldwide turnover, for at most six months. 🟢 Art 35(7)-(8)

## Threat-led penetration testing

Entities identified by their competent authority run TLPT at least every three
years, following TIBER-EU-aligned RTS. 🟢 Art 26(1), 26(8)
